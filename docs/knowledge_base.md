## Core recurring principles (top 12, ranked by how often they recur)

1. **Emotion first, then mechanics.** Start from the feeling you want and pick mechanics that produce it. Examples: Gruul as "musical chairs" [Gruul musical-chairs short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=0s) (the description calls this "approaching design with an emotion first"). Difficulty should be set by "what the player came here to feel" [How hard should your game be short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sejMX_vdHU&t=0s). Camera choice should "support the emotions" [Survival game (Seymour) jam @04:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xr0wY9LtnPU&t=285s). One of the three questions for stuck problems is "what emotional response…?" [Three questions short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GowOfSAODdQ&t=0s). The color flip in Celestial Survivors was built for the Pac-Man "tables turned" moment [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=0s). Randomness should be shaped "around the player's emotions," not math [Four ways to control randomness @02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=169s).
2. **Design for what players will actually do, not what you hope they'll do.** Iterate after playtests. Loatheb's healers healed *harder* under a tank debuff [Loatheb short @00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NksxgFq6eAg&t=32s). Skeram's mind-controlled player was ignored until it got a damage buff, and players found tells the designer never intended [Prophet Skeram short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0dR_AaEstw&t=0s) [Prophet Skeram short @02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0dR_AaEstw&t=124s). The Flappy RL bot flew over the pipes [ChatGPT makes an AI bot @03:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w7TnBIsub5M&t=232s). The pumpkin room was seen in the wrong order [Virtual Escape Room jam recap @08:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R_bWn3H6msA&t=481s). Testers' suggestions point at real problems but are often wrong as fixes [Board game insiders @23:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qG3oX9H8RAo&t=1411s).
3. **Remove the dominant/static solution so the moment stays alive.** Gruul's random Ground Slam stops pre-assigned spots [Gruul musical-chairs short @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=34s). Anubisath sentinels pick random abilities on pull [Set Monster short @02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gcwqvVa_UQM&t=130s). Patchwerk's enrage stops "bring 30 healers" [Patchwerk short @01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Jw4rsbnpk&t=101s). Gruul's soft enrage stops deaths from making the fight easier [Gruul musical-chairs short @02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=155s).
4. **Make the read instant: feedback, silhouette, and juice.** Cut anticipation frames (~180 ms) [Responsive game feel short @01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=90s). Camera shake is "salt" [Camera shake @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=67s). Dave the Diver's harpoon has 10 feedback layers [Slow-motion game feel short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xiwG4HwW8H0&t=0s). Put damage feedback where the eyes are, plus an obnoxious sound [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @12:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=771s). Visual and audio feedback teach rules [Survival game (Seymour) jam @09:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xr0wY9LtnPU&t=584s). Reinforce the core mechanic with music timing [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @03:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=202s).
5. **Random is not the same as fair; control streaks.** Marble bag, progressive probability, pity limit, predetermination [Control randomness short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=0s) [Four ways to control randomness @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=0s) [Marble bag deep dive @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKVGrWrEqLQ&t=0s). Players expect randomness to self-correct [Control randomness short @00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=33s) [Four ways to control randomness @01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=102s).
6. **Pick formulas for the curve you want and present pretty numbers.** mit = A/(A+K), where survivability grows linearly with armor [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=133s). Choose K from a target mitigation and nice per-slot numbers [Armor formula Pt 2 short @01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=69s).
7. **Design rewards for stability and awareness; beware acclimation.** Use cascading loot rolls [Loot system short @01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=116s). Magic Find suffers from no awareness, acclimation, and paralysis [Magic Find short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=0s). Optional content with weak loot gets skipped [Buru short @01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esBii7uPwcw&t=84s). Break long goals into milestones or add chance [Why games feel grindy @05:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9UpeKknwayc&t=337s).
8. **Keep attention in the moment (flow), or grind sets in** [Why games feel grindy @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9UpeKknwayc&t=34s) [Why games feel grindy @02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9UpeKknwayc&t=167s).
9. **Mechanics and fantasy reinforce each other.** Dave's oxygen and weight [Dave the Diver spotlight @05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=311s) [Dave the Diver spotlight @06:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=377s). Phone-as-UI [Dave the Diver spotlight @04:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=253s). "You become V" [Cyberpunk one-word short @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_Zkqz0NkjU&t=34s). Theme is a comprehension shortcut [Board game insiders @34:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qG3oX9H8RAo&t=2076s).
10. **Name the kind of fun precisely** (8 kinds of fun / MDA): Cuphead = challenge, It Takes Two = fellowship, Vampire Survivors = submission, Beat Saber = sensation, Dispatch = narrative, Cyberpunk = fantasy [Cuphead fun-type short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXb2jOoJ6ck&t=0s) [It Takes Two fun-type short @00:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j_b4kZJWRRs&t=55s) [Vampire Survivors one-word short @01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4vPnB4Hcss&t=60s) [Beat Saber one-word short @01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f7VkUSWYJPw&t=66s) [Dispatch one-word short @01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YVzOcvwGtK4&t=80s) [Cyberpunk one-word short @00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_Zkqz0NkjU&t=58s).
11. **Say no and protect the vision; context decides whether an idea is good.** Prioritize downstream impact [Too many good ideas short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ylcd0Yryk74&t=0s). Write down audience and experience [Good-but-wrong idea short @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Weh9o3gYGOo&t=67s). Set monsters failed in Diablo but worked in WoW [Set Monster short @02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gcwqvVa_UQM&t=162s). Starfield's fading tracker shows "what you say you won't do" [Starfield spotlight @03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYiIt5eoN-o&t=191s). Dave has no minimap [Dave the Diver spotlight @07:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=475s).
12. **Ship small and get to playable fast.** First playable at 25% of the time [Game Jam tips 1-5 @06:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3N_ikDs0WYI&t=385s) [Rhythm Runner jam recap @01:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6c-41ghE5yI&t=100s). ### 1.2 Encounter-design rules distilled (Said → cited)
- **Ask what the fight tests, and for whom:** personal responsibility for a random player [Buru short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esBii7uPwcw&t=0s); healers DPSing [Loatheb short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NksxgFq6eAg&t=0s); pure tuning across tanks, healers, and DPS [Patchwerk short @02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Jw4rsbnpk&t=136s); class utility [Razuvious short @00:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0uZi2Im2k5w&t=22s); reading and kill-order [Set Monster short @02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gcwqvVa_UQM&t=130s).
- **Health-% triggers test execution. Timers test DPS and snowball with gear** [Prophet Skeram short @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0dR_AaEstw&t=67s).
- **Hard caps beat soft penalties when soft penalties create panic or dilemmas** (Loatheb) [Loatheb short @01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NksxgFq6eAg&t=65s).
- **Use enrage timers (hard or soft) to close degenerate strategies** [Patchwerk short @01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Jw4rsbnpk&t=101s) [Gruul musical-chairs short @02:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=155s).
- **Design around network realities.** Slow movement gives clients time to sync [Gruul musical-chairs short @01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=89s). Hateful Strike picks the highest-HP off-tank to give healers reaction time [Patchwerk short @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b2Jw4rsbnpk&t=67s).
- **Suspense beats are design.** Pause before the payoff (petrify, then delay, then Shatter) [Gruul musical-chairs short @02:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LsY3FHxBVgw&t=122s).
- **Big responses to big threats.** Add a damage buff so the "right" response (CC) is actually needed [Prophet Skeram short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0dR_AaEstw&t=0s).
- **Combat loops can teach through discovery.** Razuvious's first pull wipes. Players learn by reading logs and finding the trick [Razuvious short @01:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0uZi2Im2k5w&t=89s).
- **Add visible tells, but players will find unintended ones too** [Prophet Skeram short @02:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0dR_AaEstw&t=124s).
- **Some boss-like moments in other genres:** Cuphead shows a progress bar and phase reached on death, "manufactur[ing] micro victories" [Cuphead fun-type short @00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXb2jOoJ6ck&t=49s). Celestial Survivors' pack spawns sit in a cone placed ahead of the player's movement "to trap them" [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=336s).

*Inference:* Across Cheng's bosses there's a 4-step template: (a) one-sentence emotion or test, (b) a core mechanic that creates it, (c) a counter-mechanic that removes the solved state (randomness, timers, enrages), (d) fairness patches (latency slack, reaction windows, readable tells). The GDD uses this template.

## 2. Game loops & progression

- **Inner/outer loops that combo** (Vampire Survivors). Runs earn coins, coins buy permanent stats, and stats help the next run. "The more time you spend playing, the more progress you make" [Vampire Survivors one-word short @00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4vPnB4Hcss&t=27s) [Vampire Survivors one-word short @01:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4vPnB4Hcss&t=60s).
- **Constant power growth.** "Numbers going up and to the right"; level-ups every few seconds [Vampire Survivors one-word short @00:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V4vPnB4Hcss&t=27s).
- **Core-fantasy-driven systems** (Dave the Diver). Oxygen depletes over time and on hits, and O₂ tanks show up as loot. Running out makes you pick one item to keep instead of a harsh death [Dave the Diver spotlight @05:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=311s). Weight × oxygen gives *player-controlled* risk-reward [Dave the Diver spotlight @06:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=409s) [Dave the Diver spotlight @07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=442s). A day dive / night restaurant split gives dual fantasy [Dave the Diver spotlight @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=0s).
- **Expansion structure** (Phantom Liberty, 4 steps). (1) New district. (2) A gateway that's closed if you don't own it. (3) A new self-contained progression system in a prominent UI spot (relic tree). (4) Connect them in a closed loop (relic points earned only in Dogtown). Give away anything that would force two rule sets (the skill-tree redesign) [Cyberpunk DLC formula @01:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=80s) [Cyberpunk DLC formula @04:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=247s) [Cyberpunk DLC formula @06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=413s) [Cyberpunk DLC formula @07:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=448s) [Cyberpunk DLC formula @09:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=543s). When adding a progression system, plan both its discovery and its *reminders through repeated interaction* [Cyberpunk DLC formula @06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pijN-tXX3A&t=413s).
- **Self-directed progression** (Starfield). The objective tracker fades out. Nudges include the outpost prompt, a ship engineer at each city, scanner completion counts, side-mission pop-ups with a breadcrumb if declined, crafting recipes as self-assigned missions, and skill challenges per rank (shotgun 20/50/100 kills) that link points to activity and give progress between levels. ## 3. Loot & reward systems

- **Four iterations of a loot table** [Loot system short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=0s) [Loot system short @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=34s) [Loot system short @00:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=58s) [Loot system short @01:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=91s) [Loot system short @01:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=116s):
  1. One big table with duplicate entries per rarity. Hard to maintain ("renumbering the whole table").
  2. Frequency weights. Adding 3 new common swords *dilutes* legendary rates. A hotfix disabling the scimitar *raises* legendary rates and players acclimate. Re-enabling it effectively nerfs legendaries.
  3. Roll rarity first, then roll in that rarity's subtable. Robust.
  4. **Cascading rolls (his recommendation).** Roll legendary first (e.g., 2%). On a miss, roll the next tier, and so on. "More precision and stability during live ops," because the number players care about most stays fixed. Complex games then add conditionals and nested tables [Loot system short @02:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEsdTuOt-Vk&t=147s).
- **Magic Find is "cursed"** [Magic Find short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=0s) [Magic Find short @00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=33s) [Magic Find short @01:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=66s) [Magic Find short @01:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=101s) [Magic Find short @02:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJ08SA90Cwg&t=135s):
  - Problems: (1) *no awareness*, since its effect is entangled with difficulty, kill speed, and mode (D3 tried call-outs via popup, color, or audio, which "only chips away"); (2) *acclimation*, since brains detect change and filter constants (the store → Starbucks analogy); (3) the *power vs reward trade-off is paralyzing* for many players.

- **Rewards must justify optional content** (Buru skipped) [Buru short @01:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=esBii7uPwcw&t=84s).

- **Dave the Diver:** O₂ tanks as exciting loot; weight makes greed a risk [Dave the Diver spotlight @05:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=344s) [Dave the Diver spotlight @07:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=442s).

Photon Torpedoes: full-screen and strong, but on a fixed interval with no target choice. Phaser Array: nearest same-color enemy, with a battery charge/discharge skill ceiling. Guardian Orb: kills *either* color, has the lowest DPS, and has zero range [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @06:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=402s) [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @07:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=435s) [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @07:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=468s) [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @08:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=500s) [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @08:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=533s) [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @09:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=566s).

## 4. Math & formulas

### 4.1 Armor / mitigation (Said)
- Formula: **mitigation = Armor / (Armor + K)** (stated in the description of [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=0s)). He says League of Legends, WoW, Dota 2, Warframe, and Diablo II/III/IV use it [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=0s). Diablo II expresses it through Attack Rating, which makes results probabilistic (description).
- Why not flat subtraction (StarCraft; tried first on Diablo II)? Attack-speed variance: 100 dmg/2 s vs 10 dmg ×5/s are the same DPS, but 10 armor barely touches one and zeroes the other. DoTs, ground fire, and thorns all need special rules [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=24s) [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @00:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=49s).
- Why not linear % (1 armor = 1%)? Each point gets *more* valuable (80→90% halves damage taken), so you need a hard cap [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @01:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=83s) [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @01:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=106s).
- "People say this formula has diminishing returns, but that's the wrong way to look at it… Look at how many hits you can survive." With 1,000 life and 100-dmg hits, each +100 armor = +10 hits; survivability is "directly proportional to your armor" [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=133s) [Simple Math / Armor Pt 1 short @02:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IzHAjV8YEW4&t=167s).
  - *Inference (math check):* EHP = HP / (1 − A/(A+K)) = HP·(A+K)/K, which is linear in A. His example implies K = 100.
- **Choosing K** [Armor formula Pt 2 short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=0s) [Armor formula Pt 2 short @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=34s) [Armor formula Pt 2 short @01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=69s) [Armor formula Pt 2 short @01:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=105s) [Armor formula Pt 2 short @02:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HecTqtkoWyg&t=137s): League K = 100 (100 armor → ½ damage, 200 → ⅓). Warcraft III K ≈ 17 (Archer 0, Grunt 1, Footman 2 armor: small numbers matter in short RTS matches). WoW K = 400 + 85 × *attacker* level (attacker, not player, or mitigation would drop as you level).
  - **4-step tuning:** (1) target mitigation for a fully geared player (e.g., 25%); (2) pick pretty per-slot armor gains per item level (chest/helm/legs 4; gloves/boots/shoulders 3; wrist/back 2); (3) sum them (28 per level); (4) solve for K. This gives 84 per level, ≈ WoW's 85.
  - Generalization: Genshin uses it for DEF and Elemental Mastery. Use it "anytime you want to turn an ever-increasing progression stat into a percentage."

### 4.2 Probability & randomness control (Said)
- Pure RNG is stateless: with a 25% crit, there's a 31.6% chance of no crit in 4 swings and 10% in 8, so "your math is right, but your game is still going to feel broken." "Random isn't the same as fair" [Control randomness short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=0s) [Control randomness short @02:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=138s).
- **Marble bag** (sampling without replacement, refill when empty) = "randomness with a memory." Nest bags (Piggy word game: vowel/consonant bag → per-letter bags → one "rare" marble → rare-letter bag so J/K/Q/X/Z are rare *and* spread apart). Bag size controls variance (2/6 vs 25/75). A quest needing 10 coins at 50% from a 10/10 bag guarantees finishing in ≤20 kills. Mechanically a deck; the name avoids namespace clashes [Control randomness short @00:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=33s) [Control randomness short @01:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2anY9ICA-Pg&t=69s) [Marble bag deep dive @01:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKVGrWrEqLQ&t=102s) [Marble bag deep dive @02:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKVGrWrEqLQ&t=169s) [Marble bag deep dive @04:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKVGrWrEqLQ&t=259s) [Marble bag deep dive @05:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mKVGrWrEqLQ&t=325s).
- **Four control techniques** [Four ways to control randomness @00:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=34s) [Four ways to control randomness @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=67s) [Four ways to control randomness @03:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=203s) [Four ways to control randomness @04:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=268s) [Four ways to control randomness @05:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=336s) [Four ways to control randomness @06:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=400s) [Four ways to control randomness @07:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYOzD0Sxt4A&t=467s):
  1. Marble bag (his favorite general tool). It has the highest storage cost (can be stored as seed + draw count) and matters for MMO servers.
  2. Progressive probability: 85% hit, +5% per miss, reset on hit. Matches gambler's-fallacy emotions; can lower the base rate to compensate.
  3. Fixed limit / pity: 20% fur, guaranteed by 10 kills. The most *felt* by players and gameable (9 squirrels then a grizzly), so use it where outcomes are standardized or gaming it is a feature.
  4. Predetermination: roll "kills needed" (1–10) up front. Invisible; good when there's a specific worst case to avoid.
  - Bad-luck protection is usually invisible. Avoid it in PvP ("one player's good fortune could be a negative experience for their opponent"). Skill can come from handling bad luck.

## 6. Game feel (sub-theme of psychology; heavily recurring)

- **Responsiveness over animation tradition.** Classical animation splits into anticipation / action / follow-through (thirds). Anticipation reads as input lag. Mario starts rising on the press. In Diablo III, speeding up the barbarian swing lost weight and still felt sluggish. **Chopping the anticipation frames** so the character "instantly pops" to the wound-up silhouette fixed it: "five or six frames… maybe 180 milliseconds… makes all the difference." A big silhouette change gives an instant visual read [Responsive game feel short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=0s) [Responsive game feel short @00:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=32s) [Responsive game feel short @00:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=57s) [Responsive game feel short @01:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=90s) [Responsive game feel short @02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jl7OYBo_oIU&t=123s).
- **Camera shake = salt.** Use it for emphasis on player actions and big events, not realism sway. Examples: Destiny 2 gun *and* melee; Cyberpunk smart shotgun (shake exists purely for feel); Hades (several tiny shakes per attack); Marvel Snap (card spawns and effects). Axes: rotation is the most noticeable, so use it sparingly. Horizontal should be gentle. **Recommended: vertical only, ≤0.1 s.** "If players are complaining about your camera shake, you've gone too far." He references "The Art of Screenshake" [Camera shake @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=0s) [Camera shake @01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=67s) [Camera shake @02:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=133s) [Camera shake @03:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=193s) [Camera shake @04:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=262s) [Camera shake @05:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=338s) [Camera shake @06:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lp-AnYMGpQs&t=368s).
- **Frame-by-frame study** (Dave the Diver harpoon, 10 details): aim tracks the harpoon; an aim arc shows min/max; the camera zooms in (anticipation); a lean-back on fire; fish knockback (which also guarantees a reel-in phase); blood at the impact point and on the fish; a bubble trail; kicking feet (struggle); shake while hooked; the camera eases out (closure). - **Feedback must be where the eyes are.** Players missed a health bar at the screen bottom. ## 7. Design philosophy, process & team craft

**Problem solving**
- When stuck, stop and answer three questions: (1) how would you describe the problem to a friend? (2) what emotional response do you want? (3) how did other games solve it (dialogue weight: Disco Elysium, BG3, The Walking Dead)? Then let your subconscious work [Three questions short @00:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GowOfSAODdQ&t=0s) [Three questions short @00:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GowOfSAODdQ&t=35s). Board designers agree: stop and play other games [Board game insiders @35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qG3oX9H8RAo&t=2142s).
- Critics should ask "what challenges and constraints did this dev team face" (time, resources, IP holders, legal/privacy) instead of "the devs are idiots" [Breaking Into Games podcast Ep1 @31:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZ1x5a-1RPg&t=1885s) [Breaking Into Games podcast Ep1 @32:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZ1x5a-1RPg&t=1920s) [Breaking Into Games podcast Ep1 @32:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZ1x5a-1RPg&t=1954s).
- Good design includes what you *don't* implement or change: Starfield's fading tracker, Dave's missing minimap [Starfield spotlight @03:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYiIt5eoN-o&t=191s) [Dave the Diver spotlight @07:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=475s). Remove mechanics to simplify when possible. Celestial Survivors cut XP pickups, which he later doubted [Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors lessons @02:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=re10Zp6cb1c&t=136s). - "Appeal to your core audience intensely" rather than "everybody weakly" [Starfield spotlight @12:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYiIt5eoN-o&t=774s).
- Fantasy-first brainstorm: list activities, objects, emotions, and characters for the core fantasy before deciding which become gameplay [Dave the Diver spotlight @04:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9SpGLY2iBM&t=279s). ## Gaps, caveats & unclear items

**Access**
- **All 57 videos were transcribed (57/57).** None were inaccessible. Method: yt-dlp listed the channel (26 on /videos, 31 on /shorts; /streams doesn't exist). YouTube then rate-limited the box's IP for caption downloads: yt-dlp hit "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" / HTTP 429, and youtube-transcript-api got IpBlocked. Transcripts were instead pulled from the public caption mirror youtubetotranscript.com using headless Chrome. Metadata and **full descriptions** came from yt-dlp (`android_vr` client) and are in `inventory.csv` and `raw/<id>.description`.
- **The channel matches Jeff's count exactly: 57 = 31 Shorts + 26 long-form.**
- The "armor formula parts" are two videos. Part 1 = "The Simple Math Hiding in Your Favorite Games" (IzHAjV8YEW4); Part 2 = "PART 2: How to tune the Armor formula…" (HecTqtkoWyg). Part 2's description calls Part 1 "my last video for the Armor formula."

**Caption quality**
- All transcripts are YouTube captions via a mirror. Whether each track was manual or auto-generated **couldn't be verified** (the mirror doesn't say). Several 2023 videos (Godot/ChatGPT, Twitch-chat jam, Sci-Fi Vampire Survivors, board-game interviews, escape-room let's play, game-jam tips) clearly have **unpunctuated auto-captions**. Newer Shorts look punctuated (possibly manual or improved ASR).
- Name garbling: "Bruth/Brute" = Buru; "Scaram" = Skeram; "Candalson/candle sun/handles on" = candlesan; "Brian Kendrickan" = Brian Kindregan; "Vlad Bîr" = the Vlambeer "Art of Screenshake" talk (attributing it to Jan Willem Nijman is **my inference**); "Reiner/Ryder" = the dog; "Piggy Pow / Piggy Pile-up / Piggy Powup" = the same word-game jam, real title unclear; jam teammates "Mike Yakan", "Drayzun/Drazen", "Lar Panda" may be mis-spelled; board-game guest names ("Steven ahrensberger", "John declare") are auto-caption spellings (likely Steven Aramini and John D.
