Fight setup

Tier: 1

How to play

  • WASD move · Mouse aim · Left click / J swing · Space dash · Right click / K shove
  • Every 10 s (16 beats) the music stops: a slam throws you somewhere random, time slows, chairs appear, the Ushers race you for them. Freeze… then SHATTER hits everyone without a chair.
  • Shove or kill an Usher to steal its chair. Boss changes phase at 66% and 33%. +15% boss damage every 30 s.
